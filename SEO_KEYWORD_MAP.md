# Sarkari Sarthi keyword map

Updated: 2026-11-18  
Market: India. The portal is currently English-language; keep Hindi search terms as a future content opportunity until Hindi pages and support are maintained.

## Primary page targets

| Page / section | Primary phrase | Supporting phrases |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | latest government jobs in India | Sarkari Naukri, latest govt jobs 2026, government job notifications, sarkari job vacancy |
| Medical category | medical government jobs | medical recruitment, hospital jobs, healthcare government vacancies |
| Engineering category | engineering government jobs | government engineering vacancies, technical recruitment |
| Teaching category | teaching government jobs | teacher recruitment, school and education vacancies |
| Administrative category | administrative government jobs | government administrative vacancies, office recruitment |

## Inventory and source-quality guardrails

Target category phrases only when the live job manifest contains current records. Do not describe all listings as officially verified or claim that every listing comes directly from a government website. Show per-record source and verification labels and direct applicants to confirm the original recruiting authority notice.

## Deliberately excluded terms

Do not target Sarkari Result, admit card download, answer key, exam result, syllabus or MP government jobs as product features until those pages and data are genuinely supported.

## Measurement

Use Search Console query and page reports to compare impressions, clicks, CTR and average position after deployment. Numeric search volume, CPC and difficulty are not invented.
