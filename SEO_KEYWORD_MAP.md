## Inventory and source-quality guardrails

The current manifest snapshot contains 227 records: 201 active and 26 expired. Active category counts include Medical (49), Engineering (21), Administrative (22) and Teaching (7). Banking (3), Railway (2), SSC (1), Defence (1) and UPSC (1) are too sparse for standalone programmatic landing pages at this time.

The current verification mix is 199 active records marked `secondary_source_only` and 2 marked `official_verified`. Do not describe all listings as officially verified or claim that every listing comes directly from a government website. The portal should show source and verification labels and direct applicants to confirm the original recruiting authority notice.

## Deliberately excluded terms

Do not target `Sarkari Result`, `admit card download`, `answer key`, `exam result`, `syllabus` or `MP government jobs` as product features until those pages and data are genuinely supported. The current inventory snapshot contains no Madhya Pradesh-specific job records and the portal is primarily a recruitment-listing experience.

## Research and measurement notes

- Phrase selection uses current search-result/competitor page language, the portal's actual category inventory and the user's Search Console property context.
- The connected Search Console opportunity report did not expose enough query-level data for a reliable Sarkari Sarthi query-volume estimate.
- Numeric search volume, CPC and difficulty are not invented. The connected keyword-research account had only 5 credits remaining, and a full keyword-metrics batch would exceed that balance.
- Use Search Console after deployment to compare impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by query and landing page. Create additional category hubs only when the live inventory is large enough to support useful content.
